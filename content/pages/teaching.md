Description: Teaching by Haining Wang at Indiana University: biomedical data science with NIH All of Us data, practical large language models, music data mining, and the free Under Five lecture and lab series.

I teach students to apply statistical and machine learning methods to real-world data, from NIH *All of Us* records to large language models.

<br>

### Courses Taught

* *DSCI-D 590 Biomedical Data Science in Practice*, Indiana University (online, Summer 2025, Fall 2025, and Spring 2026)  
    - A new course applying AI and biostatistical methods using NIH *All of Us* data. Co-designed and co-taught with Drs. Haixu Tang and Jing Su in the summer; co-taught with Dr. Jiangqiong Li in the fall and spring.

* *ILS-Z 604 Music Data Mining*, Indiana University (in person, Fall 2022)  
    - Led lab sessions on machine and deep learning for audio and text analysis.

### Workshops

### Under Five Series

Free lectures and labs on computational methods, open to anyone. The name nods to von Neumann's quip, as Fermi told it to Freeman Dyson ([Dyson, 2004](https://doi.org/10.1038/427297a)): "With four parameters I can fit an elephant, and with five I can make him wiggle his trunk." Fitting is easy; understanding is the point. [Watch the series on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLamS_Ppdju2s).
